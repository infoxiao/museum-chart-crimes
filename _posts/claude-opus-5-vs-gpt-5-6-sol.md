[Tibo (@thsottiaux)](https://x.com/thsottiaux) called it out:

> “Can you zoom in more on the y axis please”

The chart compares Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, but its y-axis covers only 58 to 61. That narrow range makes a difference of less than two points look much larger than it is.

Starting the y-axis at zero would put the gap in proportion to the full scale of the metric.
